Create identity (set your passphrase)

        

          You choose the passphrase - it locks your keys with Argon2id. It is never stored or
          sent anywhere, and it cannot be recovered: if you lose it, the keys and the sealed
          data addressed to them are lost.
        

        
        
        
        
        


        
Signets on this device

        

          One Signet per person, several identities inside for context
          (alice-home, alice-business). A Signet is one
          datu wallet serve process: its own directory, passphrases and token.
          The panel never holds keys - it asks the bridge to sign and decrypt.
        

        
        

          
          
        

        
        
        
        

          
          
        

        

          
        

        


        
Backup & move devices

        

          Exports the selected identity as a file encrypted with a separate backup
          passphrase. Import it on another device - the restored identity unlocks with
          that backup passphrase.
